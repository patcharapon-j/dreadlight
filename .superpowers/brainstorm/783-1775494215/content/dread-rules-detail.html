Dread Rules — Detailed Mechanics

How Dread rises and falls as an independent track

Gaining Dread

The GM assigns Dread based on campaign-defined triggers. The following are examples and guidance, not a fixed table — the GM decides what "Dread" means for their campaign.

Example Trigger Suggested Gain Notes
Minor exposure (strange omen, unsettling scene)+1The baseline nudge
Significant encounter (entity contact, forbidden knowledge)+1–2Core horror moments
Major transgression (supernatural bargain, crossing a line)+2–3Player-driven escalation
Voluntary acceptance (player chooses to embrace the dread)+1Player agency — trade safety for power

💡 Campaign flexibility: In a psychological thriller, these might be "witness a betrayal (+1)", "commit violence (+2)". In survival horror, "enter unknown territory (+1)", "use the last supplies (+1)".

Losing Dread

Two paths to relief: safe scenes (passive, narrative) and player actions (active, resource-spending).

🏠 Scene-Based Relief

  • Safe haven scene: −1 Dread when a scene is explicitly safe and the characters decompress
  • Cathartic moment: −1 Dread for a breakthrough, confession, or moment of genuine peace
  • Extended downtime: Reset Dread to 0 between scenarios (weeks/months of normalcy)

🎯 Player Actions

  • Anchor interaction: −1 Dread when engaging with your Anchor (once per session)
  • Crack: −1 Dread when you voluntarily introduce a complication from your fears/trauma (once per scene)
  • Confront the source: −1 Dread when you face what's causing the dread head-on (GM discretion)

Interaction with Existing Mechanics

Mechanic Interaction with Dread
Dice poolsUnchanged — Dread dice replace base dice, expand pool if Dread > attribute
PushingUnchanged — dread 1s cause Soul loss, base 1s cause Mind loss
Soul trackNow purely a health pool. Losing Soul no longer affects Dread.
Soul recoveryUnchanged — still the slowest track. No longer reduces Dread.
Severing ContactRecovers all Soul AND resets Dread to 0 (both benefits preserved)
GM Dread PoolFully separate. No cross-contamination.
CracksNow reduce Dread (−1) in addition to recovering Mind (+1)
AnchorNow reduces Dread (−1) in addition to recovering Mind (+1)
Marks / SpiralUnchanged — still tracks corruption independently

Resolve Talent Interaction

Resolve L3 (Iron Soul) still works as written — it doesn't reduce Dread directly, but lets you suppress one Dread Die for a single roll, once per session. It remains a coping mechanism. Since Dread now has its own reduction paths, Iron Soul is less critical but still valuable for clutch moments.
