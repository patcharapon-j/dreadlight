Changes in v2

  • Large frameless portrait — Character image fills the entire header with gradient fade-out on edges. Name/concept/tags overlay the bottom.
  • Tactile track buttons — Body, Mind, Soul, Supply are icon+label buttons rather than raw numbers. Clickable, hoverable, with glow feedback.
  • Dread as pips — Five dark squares filled/empty rather than a number. Visceral.
  • Merged attribute + condition — Each attribute cell shows its paired condition. Inactive = ghosted text. Active = red highlight with −2 indicator. The "Shaken" on INS example shows how a condition visually degrades the attribute.
  • Monaspace Krypton — GitHub's monospace font throughout. Labels use letter-spacing for the terminal/sci-fi feel.
