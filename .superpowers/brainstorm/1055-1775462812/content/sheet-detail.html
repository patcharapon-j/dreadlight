Design Elements from Ironroll

  • Geometric corner brackets — framing the sheet edges
  • Gold accent lines — top/bottom horizontal rules with gradient fade
  • Deep dark surfaces — #08080c base, #111118 cards, #0e0e14 header
  • Track-specific colors — Body green, Mind blue, Soul purple, Dread dark grey
  • Hover glow on clickable elements — attributes and talents glow on hover
  • Monospace typography — labels with letter-spacing for that terminal/sci-fi feel
  • Subtle borders — thin lines at very low opacity, brighter for active/selected
  • Talent level pips — filled/empty squares showing progression
  • Occult talent highlighted in purple to signal its Soul-cost nature
  • Spiral mark pips — filled circles with track colors, final pip marked with ✕
